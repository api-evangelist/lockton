# Lockton

> Lockton Companies is the world's largest privately held, independent insurance brokerage, founded by Jack Lockton in 1966 and headquartered in Kansas City, Missouri. Lockton is a broker and intermediary rather than a risk carrier, placing commercial property and casualty, specialty and financial lines, employee benefits, private client coverage, and — through Lockton Re — treaty and facultative reinsurance across more than 140 offices worldwide.

## API posture

Lockton has NO public API. As of July 2026 there is no developer portal, no API
reference, no OpenAPI/Swagger definition, no GraphQL endpoint, no gRPC/protobuf,
no public Postman collection, no webhook or event catalog, and no MCP server.
The hostnames developer.lockton.com, developers.lockton.com, docs.lockton.com,
api.lockton.com and apis.lockton.com do not resolve in DNS. A crawl of all 6,953
URLs in global.lockton.com/sitemap.xml returns zero developer, API, ACORD, IVANS,
OpenAPI or Swagger paths.

Client-facing technology is delivered through authenticated regional Lockton
Client Portals and the Lockton Re SAGE intelligence and analytics platform. SAGE
runs as an authenticated Angular application at sage.lockton.com behind Azure
Front Door; it is presented on the public website behind a contact form with no
self-serve access, no documented data feed and no published integration surface.

This is the expected posture for a US broker-intermediary. The United States has
no federal insurance regulator and no open-insurance mandate, so broker/carrier
data exchange runs on ACORD standards in an EDI-and-forms idiom rather than a
public API idiom. Lockton's most substantive standards signal is governance
rather than implementation: in July 2026 Nidhi Howell, Chief Business Technology
Officer of Lockton Re, was appointed to the ACORD Solutions Group Board of
Directors representing the reinsurance broking channel. No ACORD AL3, ACORD XML,
NGDS or IVANS agency-download implementation is documented on any public Lockton
property.

## Notes for agents

There is nothing here to call programmatically. Do not attempt to construct
Lockton API requests — no base URL, no authentication scheme and no operation
is published. Engagement with Lockton runs through a broker relationship and
the authenticated Client Portal, not through a self-serve developer surface.
